## Purpose and fees

[Catalonia’s Article 66 bis](https://www.boe.es/eli/es-ct/l/2025/12/29/11) distinguishes study-related housing from exclusively recreational / holiday use and calls for documented purpose. The [current consolidated LAU](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1994-26003), updated by Real Decreto-ley 29/2026, addresses justified temporary displacement in Article 2(3) and prohibits passing management / contract-formalization charges to tenants in Article 20(2). Purpose, contract and fee wording must be checked together. Advertised agency charges are retained as source claims and disputed quote items, rather than silently assumed payable or zero.
