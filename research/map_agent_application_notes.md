## 1. Executive Summary & Application Strategy

Securing a 4-bedroom furnished apartment in Barcelona for a 90-day winter exchange term requires navigating Catalonia's mid-term rental regulations (*arrendamiento de temporada* governed by Article 3.2 of Spain's *Ley de Arrendamientos Urbanos* - LAU).

### Key Strategic Rules for Success:
1. **The "Justification of Temporality" Rule:** Under Spanish law, landlords are legally prohibited from signing a standard residential contract (*vivienda habitual*, 5–7 years) for an exchange stay. Every candidate landlord and platform will require formal proof that the stay is strictly academic, temporary, and that the students maintain their permanent habitual residence in their home countries.
2. **Speed & First-Mover Advantage:** Well-priced 4-bedroom flats within a 15-minute walk of UPF Ciutadella (such as Carrer de Sardenya at €1,950/mo or Arc de Triomf at €2,000/mo) receive multiple applications. Having a pre-assembled PDF dossier with all 4 students' documents allows instant approval.
3. **Platform Booking Protection vs. Direct Agency:**
   * **Spotahome & HousingAnywhere:** Provide 24-hour move-in protection (funds held in escrow until 24 hours after check-in).
   * **Direct Agencies (aTemporal, ShBarcelona):** Require direct wire transfers of deposit and first month's rent. Direct contracts must be reviewed against Catalan regional deposit regulations (*Incasòl*).

## 4. Spanish Seasonal Rental Legal Protection & Red Flag Guide

### 1. Security Deposit (*Fianza*) Limits
* **The Legal Rule:** Under Article 36.1 of the Spanish *Ley de Arrendamientos Urbanos* (LAU), the mandatory deposit for leases other than habitual housing (*arrendamiento para uso distinto del de vivienda*) is **strictly capped at two (2) months' base rent**.
* **Red Flag:** If a landlord or unverified agency demands 3, 4, or 5 months of refundable deposit, this is irregular and increases cash risk.
* **Catalan Deposit Registry (*Incasòl*):** By Catalan law, landlords are legally obligated to deposit the security deposit with the *Institut Català del Sòl* (Incasòl). You may request the Incasòl deposit registration certificate (*resguard de dipòsit*) within 30 days of signing.

### 2. Agency & Booking Fees (*Honorarios de Agencia*)
* Under Spain's *Ley 12/2023 por el Derecho a la Vivienda* and *Real Decreto-ley 29/2026*, agency management fees for residential housing cannot be charged to tenants. However, mid-term seasonal rentals (*alquiler de temporada*) are occasionally subjected to "tenant administrative/service fees."
* **Best Practice:** When booking via Spotahome or HousingAnywhere, the platform fee is clearly itemized upfront. Direct agencies like ShBarcelona or aTemporal may charge between €500 and €1,000 as a contract drafting fee. Ensure this is explicitly agreed upon before wiring funds.

### 3. Utility Caps & Winter Heating Clause (*Suministros*)
* **Normal Winter Costs:** For 4 adult students in Barcelona in January and February (running central radiators or heat pumps, hot water, and washing machine), electricity, gas, and water typically total **€200 to €250 per month**.
* **Predatory Utility Caps:** Be cautious of contracts advertising "Rent includes utilities up to €80/month" with steep overage penalty rates. An €80 cap for 4 adults will almost certainly be exceeded during winter, resulting in unexpected deductions from the security deposit.
* **Invoice Requirement:** Insist that any overage charge must be backed by official utility utility invoices (*facturas emitidas por la compañía suministradora: Endesa, Naturgy, Aigües de Barcelona*) reflecting actual meter readings.

### 4. Move-In Inventory & Inspection (*Acta de Entrega de Llaves e Inventario*)
* Within the first 24 to 48 hours of check-in, conduct a walk-through. Take timestamped photos and videos of all existing scuffs, appliances, radiator operation, and furniture.
* Send this digital inventory by email to the landlord/agency within 48 hours to prevent wrongful deposit deductions at move-out.
