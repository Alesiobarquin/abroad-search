The schedule has a lower €165 enrollment tier for stays under three months. If RESA applies it to the exact 89- or 86-night stay, this comparison falls to €10,179.20, or €848.27/person/month. That treatment is unconfirmed. Kitchenware, laundry tariffs, electricity reconciliation, first-day bedding setup and four-room allocation remain open. Admission favors first-year UPF undergraduates and full-year stays. The [student studio description](https://resa.es/es/residencias/barcelona/la-ciutadella/) explicitly excludes cookware/tableware, so this is not yet a move-in-ready recommendation.
