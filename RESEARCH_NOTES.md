## Budget and map methods

The hard cap is €11,040 for a three-month comparison, excluding refundable deposits. Monthly per-person comparison = occupancy subtotal / 3 / 4. If utilities are not included or unclear, the winter provision is €250/month for four, unless a more specific conservative scenario is recorded. Missing agency, booking, cleaning or tax charges stay unknown; a low subtotal is not a verified budget fit. USD uses the original budget’s implied rate, not a live conversion.

Jan 1–Mar 31 is 89 nights and Jan 5–Apr 1 is 86 nights. Three calendar-month calculations are comparison scenarios, not exact prorated invoices. Check whether an April 1 departure adds another billed calendar month.

Building-number geocodes are distinguished from inherited platform points and street / station proxies. A–C, H and I use explicit proxies because their house numbers are unknown. B’s long street and I’s very long street make exact commute checks particularly important. D’s house-number search returned only unrelated street segments; those results were rejected and no pin is shown. Unlocated findings stay visible in the list. E’s 23.92-minute and F’s 50.08-minute walks are saved OpenStreetMap routed-foot estimates to the supplied campus anchor; no direct transit route for F has been established. Clicking a route opens a fresh map search rather than asserting an exact journey.

## Legal and fee uncertainty

The [Spanish tax agency’s rental guidance](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/iva-operaciones-inmobiliarias/alquilo-apartamento-turistico-tengo-que-iva.html) distinguishes accommodation with hotel-type services from rentals without them. A stay over 31 nights alone does not establish VAT exemption. Entry / exit cleaning alone is not treated as a hotel-type service in that guidance.

The referenced [Real Decreto-ley 29/2026 record](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823) was located, but its full consolidated effect on each seasonal agency-fee arrangement was not established. No unknown agency fee has been assumed waived. Ask for the full non-refundable fee schedule and an academic-purpose lease before committing.
